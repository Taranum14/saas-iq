How it works: next month's MRR is this month's MRR times the share of revenue kept, plus the MRR from new customers. Revenue kept is the six-month average. New MRR follows a 12-month straight-line trend. Because the two pieces are separate, the model can answer "what if churn improves?", which a single straight line through past MRR cannot.
