Who should we call this week?

The model estimates each paying customer's chance of cancelling next month. A call costs money, so customers are ranked by what a call is worth: chance of leaving, times chance we keep them, times the margin we keep, minus the cost of the call.

These four numbers are assumptions, not measurements. A real team would learn them from an A/B test: call a random half of the at-risk customers and compare. Change them to see how the plan shifts.
